When managed UX research makes sense

You don't have internal research capacity

Your team doesn't have a dedicated UX researcher, and the product or design team is stretched. You need someone to take the research off your plate — not just advise on methodology.

You want an outside perspective

An external team brings different eyes to familiar problems. When internal researchers are too close to the product, or when organizational dynamics complicate objectivity, external research has real value.

You need research that drives a decision

There's a specific question — about a feature, a workflow, a product direction, an unmet need. You need quality research findings that stakeholders will trust and act on, not a research report that gets filed away.

You're scoping a new product area

Discovery research before a new initiative, a market you're entering, or an audience you're not deeply familiar with. Getting research right at the start is far cheaper than building the wrong thing.

How it works

What a managed engagement looks like

Every engagement is scoped to the actual problem. Here's the general shape of how we work.

Scope the research question

We start with a conversation about what you're trying to learn and what decisions the research needs to support. Good research starts with the right question — not a methods plan.

Design the approach

We choose methods appropriate to the question: generative interviews, usability testing, concept evaluation, diary studies, survey design, competitive analysis. We explain the trade-offs and what each approach can and cannot tell you.

Recruit the right participants

Participant quality makes or breaks research. We handle recruiting with appropriate screening for your target population — not just whoever is convenient.

Conduct and synthesize

We run the research. Interviews, sessions, analysis. We use AI-assisted tools where appropriate to accelerate processing — but synthesis, interpretation, and recommendations are always human-driven. Patterns, tensions, mental models, implications for product decisions.

Deliver something useful

Findings, recommendations, and supporting materials in a format your team can actually use. Not just data — evidence that connects to product decisions and stakeholder conversations.

Good managed research doesn't mean we disappear and return with a deck. We work closely with your team throughout — sharing early findings, adjusting as we learn, and ensuring the research stays connected to your actual decisions.

Not sure which to choose?

Agency model vs. embedded researcher

The right structure depends on the nature of the work, how long you need support, and how integrated you want research to be with your team's day-to-day.

Choose agency / managed when:
You have a defined project with a clear deliverable
You want an outside perspective on familiar problems
You don't have a research function internally
You need research ownership, not just research hours
Timeline is defined and outcomes are specific

Choose embedded / fractional when:
You need ongoing research capability, not one project
You want a researcher integrated into your team
Research is part of your regular product process
