Engagement models

Structured around your actual need

Most UX research needs fit one of these structures. We'll help you figure out which applies.

Embedded staffing

A researcher joins your team much like an internal hire — attending standups, working in your tools, embedded in your product rhythm. Generally structured hourly. We handle the operating relationship throughout the engagement.

Learn more

Fractional research

Reserved capacity — a defined number of hours or days per week or month. Effective when research needs are real but don't justify a full-time placement. Clear hours, fixed availability, real accountability.

Fractional details

Managed UX research

We own the project. From research planning through synthesis, recommendations, and delivery. Good for organizations without internal capacity, teams that want an outside perspective, or situations where you need someone to take real ownership.

Direct hire recruiting

You need a permanent UX researcher. We recruit directly into your organization, using the same access and judgment we apply to contract and fractional placements. Structured as a contingency engagement.

Behind the work

Turtle builds the people layer

Experience

Nearly a decade of talent market knowledge

Josh Zak and the Turtle team have spent years mapping the research talent landscape — building relationships with researchers across qualitative, quantitative, and mixed-methods disciplines, across industries and organizational sizes.

Judgment

Vetting that goes beyond the portfolio

Research work is often confidential. We evaluate professional environments, problem-solving depth, research ownership, methodological range, autonomy, communication, and whether a researcher instills genuine confidence.
