UX Research TO

UX Research TO was a Toronto-based community event series connecting UX researchers, hiring teams, and practitioners in the local product community. The series ran through Turtle and UXResearcher.com, serving as one of the early expressions of our commitment to the UX research community.

About UX Research TO

UX Research TO brought together UX researchers, hiring leaders, and practitioners in Toronto for talks, discussions, and community building around the craft and business of UX research. Events explored topics including research methodologies, building research functions, the research talent market, and how organizations can invest more effectively in research capability.

The series reflected Turtle and UXResearcher.com's broader interest in the research community — not just as a talent marketplace, but as a place where knowledge, relationships, and standards of craft matter. Research is a community in a meaningful sense: practitioners share methods, organizations share practices, and the field advances through conversation.

The event series is on hiatus as UXResearcher.com focuses its energy on the commercial and talent matching work. If there's interest in future events or community initiatives, we'd want to hear about it.

Toronto's UX research community

Toronto has a substantial and active UX research community — researchers at major tech companies, product-led organizations, healthcare and regulated industries, and consultancies. The talent market is deep relative to the city's profile, and UX Research TO was in part an effort to increase its visibility and cohesion.

UXResearcher.com maintains an active interest in Toronto-based research talent through uxresearch.ca and our broader network — if you're a researcher or organization in the Toronto market, we're relevant to you whether or not the events resume.
