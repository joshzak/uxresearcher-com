About us

UXResearcher.com, powered by Turtle

UXResearcher.com is the research-specific expression of Turtle's flexible talent and service model. We help organizations acquire excellent UX research capability — and we help experienced researchers find work that matches their skills. Behind both sides is nearly a decade of experience in the research talent market.

The operating company

Built on Turtle's talent infrastructure

Turtle is a product talent company. We've spent years finding, evaluating, placing, and working alongside senior product talent — designers, researchers, product managers, and engineers — at organizations from ambitious startups to complex enterprise environments.

UXResearcher.com draws on that infrastructure — the talent relationships, the evaluation experience, the domain knowledge — and focuses it specifically on UX research. The category has its own dynamics, its own hiring challenges, and its own market. It deserves a dedicated property.

Turtle client experience includes organizations across consumer technology, regulated industries (healthcare, financial services), gaming, enterprise SaaS, and professional services — including Binance, Epic Games, LifeLabs / Quest Diagnostics, TELUS, World AI, and Pedalheads.

Leadership

Our position

What we actually think

Research quality is not uniform

The range between a strong UX researcher and an average one is enormous — and the difference often isn't visible on a CV or in a portfolio. Getting the match right is hard, and it matters significantly more than most hiring processes account for.

Flexibility isn't a compromise

Embedded contract, fractional, managed research — these aren't lesser alternatives to permanent headcount. For many organizations, they're better fits. The right structure for research capacity depends on the actual need, not on what feels most like "hiring."

Long relationships are the model

The initial engagement is rarely where the real value is. Organizations that invest in research well — and find capable researchers who fit — often develop relationships that persist, expand, and create significant value over time. That's the model we're optimizing for.

Access is built through engagement

We don't publish a network size. Our access comes from years of ongoing engagement with research professionals — building relationships, maintaining them, and developing an active view of who is strong, who is becoming available, and who would fit a particular environment.

Vetting requires real conversations

Research work is confidential. Portfolios don't tell you whether someone is a strong interviewer, a rigorous synthesist, or someone who navigates stakeholders effectively. Finding out requires real conversations — and the judgment to evaluate what you're hearing.

We're honest about what we don't do

We don't have a proprietary methodology. We don't publish fabricated placement speed guarantees. We don't invent case studies from confidential work. The credibility is real or it isn't — and we'd rather be straightforward about both sides of that.
